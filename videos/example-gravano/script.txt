February 24th, 2000. Phoenix.
The most famous trader in mafia history is asleep in a secret apartment 9 miles from his family, surrounded by guns. Two conspiracies hold his address. One carries federal warrants and it is rolling toward his doors right now.
The other flew in under bought names with Gambino money behind it and three guns waiting in the desert. Months of surveillance done, its shooting plan in the final stages. If the warrants don't take Sammy the Bull Gravano this morning, the guns get their window.
